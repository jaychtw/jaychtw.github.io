# Bookshelf authoring

Compose comic panels for portrait reading and supply the artwork without dialogue, captions, or choice labels. Add the English and Traditional Chinese wording to the panel or choice in library.js. Both languages use the same illustration. The reader displays the selected wording as selectable HTML text.

English sound effects such as THUD may stay in the artwork. Where a sound carries story information, describe it in the panel’s localized alt text.

The shelf follows the Possibility catalog’s card layout. Provide a separate 16:9 cover or choose an image that survives that crop. Episode artwork can use other proportions.
